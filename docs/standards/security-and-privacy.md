# Security and privacy

- Apply least privilege to users, permission sets, service principals, and integrations.
- Never store secrets in source control, app packages, logs, or documentation.
- Validate and authorize inputs at trust boundaries; do not rely on client-side checks.
- Minimize personal data, document purpose and retention, and use approved handling procedures.
- Protect sensitive information in telemetry and test fixtures; use synthetic test data.
- Follow the private reporting process in `../../SECURITY.md` for vulnerabilities.
